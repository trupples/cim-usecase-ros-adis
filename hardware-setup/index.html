    1. Hardware setup

    1.1. Bill of materials

    • 1 Raspberry Pi, one of:

      • Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, recommend at least 2 GB RAM

      • Raspberry Pi 5, recommend at least 2 GB RAM

    • 1 EVAL-ADISIMU1-RPIZ HAT for ADIS series IMUs

    • 1 ADIS16607 IMU

    • 1 microSD card, at least 16 GB

    • 1 Raspberry Pi Power Supply (5V 3A USB Type-C)

    • 1 Ribbon cable:

      • 2x8 IDC connector, 2mm contact pitch

      • Length: We recommend 20cm or shorter, to avoid signal integrity issues
